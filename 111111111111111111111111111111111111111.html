About Us

WHO WE
ARE

Blackout Riot is Lucknow's home for unforgettable, all-ages, alcohol-free Gen-Z nights — curated sets, close crowds, and a vibe you won't find anywhere else in the city.

We started Blackout Riot to build the kind of night out that didn't really exist in Lucknow yet — small, curated, all-ages events where the music and the crowd actually matter more than anything else. No alcohol, no chaos, just a tight-knit room of people who came to have a good time.

Every event is capacity-capped and checked at the door, so it stays intimate instead of turning into a mob. Entry is ticketed and verified against a guest list, with a scannable QR code tied to your name for a smooth, single-entry check-in.

From lineups to venues to the little details in between, we handle it all — so you just have to show up.
